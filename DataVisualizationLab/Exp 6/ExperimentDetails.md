Experiment No. 6 Data Aggregation and Statistical Functions in Tableau

Aim:
To perform data aggregation and apply statistical functions in Tableau for better data analysis.

Description:
Data aggregation combines multiple records into summary values. Common statistical measures
include sum, average, minimum and maximum. Tableau can perform these operations directly on
data fields.

Uses:
• Finding total sales.
• Calculating average values.
• Finding minimum and maximum values.
• Comparing categories.
• Summarizing large datasets.

Algorithm:
1. Open Tableau Public.
2. Connect to the dataset.
3. Drag required fields into rows and columns.
4. Apply aggregation functions.
5. Use statistical measures.
6. Analyze and save the results.
