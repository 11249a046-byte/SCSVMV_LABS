Experiment No. 10  Storytelling in Tableau

Aim
To create a story in Tableau by combining multiple visualizations into a sequence of insights.

Description:
Data storytelling is the process of presenting data through a sequence of visualizations and explanations. Tableau Stories allow users to arrange different visualizations as story points so that viewers can understand the analysis step by step.

Uses:
• Presenting analytical findings clearly.
• Explaining trends and patterns step by step.
• Combining charts into a logical sequence.
• Supporting business presentations.
• Communicating insights to non-technical users.

Algorithm:
1. Open Tableau Public.
2. Connect to the dataset.
3. Create multiple worksheets.
4. Create a dashboard if required.
5. Open the Story tab.
6. Add story points.
7. Arrange visualizations sequentially.
8. Add captions for explanation.
9. Analyze and save the story.
